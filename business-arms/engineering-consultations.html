Our Services

From an engineering question to a clear path forward

Our consulting services help clients define technical requirements, evaluate options, develop processes and materials, assess feasibility, and prepare a clear path from an engineering question to implementation.

Chemical & Process Engineering

Process analysis and development; process optimization; chemical-engineering calculations and technical review; operating-condition assessment; and support for laboratory, pilot, and industrial process requirements.

Materials & Product Consulting

Materials selection and evaluation; polymer and composite guidance; material-performance review; product-development support; and recommendations connecting material requirements with processing and application needs.

Technical & Feasibility Studies

Technical feasibility assessment; option comparison; preliminary process and technology review; requirement definition; risk and constraint identification; and decision-support recommendations for research and industrial initiatives.

Technical Evaluation & Project Support

Technical evaluation of proposed solutions, equipment, suppliers, and project approaches; specification review; vendor-document review; commissioning support; and coordination of technical requirements across project stakeholders.

Research & Development Engineering

Engineering support for experiments, proof-of-concept work, scale-up planning, test-method development, technical problem solving, and the translation of research outcomes into practical development pathways.

Engagement Model

A five-step process

1

Define the challenge and intended outcome.

2

Review available data, constraints, and technical requirements.

3

Develop the study, evaluation, or engineering approach.

4

Present findings, options, and recommendations.

5

Support the next stage through technology, development, or manufacturing when required.

Deliverables may include technical assessments, engineering studies, feasibility inputs, requirement documents, option comparisons, materials or process recommendations, development plans, technical-review comments, and implementation roadmaps — depending on the agreed project scope.
