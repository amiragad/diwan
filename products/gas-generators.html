Manufacturer: Young In Chromass · Country of origin: South Korea

Whisper Nitrogen Generator (External Compressor) is a laboratory nitrogen gas generator from Young In Chromass for LC/MS supply and solvent-evaporation applications. Its configuration supports high-purity nitrogen delivery, pressure control and instrument gas integration; final capacity must be selected for the connected analyzer.
