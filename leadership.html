Leadership

Dr. Mansour Albareeki

Founder & Chief Executive Officer, DIWAN Engineering & Technology Company

Founder & CEO
Dr. Mansour Albareeki

Credentials

PhD, Plastics Engineering — University of Massachusetts Lowell

3 Graduate Certificates — Plastics Materials, Plastics Processing, Commercial Development for Plastics Engineers

Advisory Council Member — Scientific & Engineering Departments, Jubail Industrial College

Registered, Saudi Center for Engineering Arbitration — classified as Expert in Petrochemical Engineering

Dr. Mansour Albareeki is a Saudi researcher, engineer, and technology entrepreneur specializing in advanced materials development and plastics and polymer engineering. His work focuses on taking materials from the molecular level to the final product through synthesis, characterization, processing, and manufacturing.

His experience combines scientific research, industrial development, sustainability, and business development. At DIWAN, he leads strategic direction, technical partnerships, and projects that connect materials science, engineering knowledge, advanced technologies, and industrial applications.

Career Timeline

2016 – 2019

Researcher in nanomanufacturing and polymer processing while pursuing his doctorate.

2019 – 2020

Postdoctoral researcher, Center for Refining and Petrochemicals, King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals. His published research covers polymer processing, multiscrew extrusion, polymer composites and nanocomposites, and the effects of manufacturing conditions on material properties and energy consumption.

2020 – 2022

Industrial Sector Manager, Saudi Top Plastic Factory — developing industrial processes and production lines, establishing laboratory procedures for recycled-material testing and quality control, and advancing mechanical and chemical polymer recycling, including applications that converted polyethylene waste and other low-value materials into higher-value products.

2022

Founded DIWAN, leading its strategic direction, technical partnerships, and projects — building a company that connects reactor systems, analytical instruments, material processing, manufacturing, and final products.

2023 – 2025

Business Manager – Sustainability, Ingenia Polymers, with regional responsibility covering the Middle East, North Africa, and Pakistan — combining technical evaluation and business development across recycled materials, material quality, processing and recycling lines, process optimization, and market requirements.

Technical Vision

His vision positions DIWAN across the full materials-development cycle: molecular level; synthesis and reaction; chemical and thermal characterization; formulation and process development; material processing; manufacturing and scale-up; and final product / application.

"Through DIWAN, Dr. Albareeki brings together research, engineering, technology access, industrial development, and commercialization to convert scientific knowledge into practical solutions, products, and manufacturing capabilities from the molecular level to the final product."
